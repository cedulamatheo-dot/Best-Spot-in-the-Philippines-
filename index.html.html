Boracay

Boracay is famous for its beautiful white sand beaches and crystal-clear turquoise water. Visitors can enjoy swimming, island hopping, water activities, and beautiful sunsets.

Puerto Princesa Underground River

The Puerto Princesa Underground River is one of Palawan's most impressive natural attractions. Visitors can ride a boat through a limestone cave and see amazing rock formations.

Chocolate Hills

The Chocolate Hills are one of the most famous attractions in Bohol. Hundreds of unusual cone-shaped hills cover the landscape and become brown during the dry season.

Mayon Volcano

Mayon Volcano is known for its almost perfectly symmetrical cone shape. Located in Albay, it is one of the most recognizable natural landmarks in the Philippines.

Siargao

Siargao is a popular island destination known for surfing, beautiful beaches, lagoons, and palm trees. It is a great destination for travelers who enjoy nature and outdoor activities.
